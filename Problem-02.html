Careers at TechNova

TechNova is a growing technology company creating innovative software and digital solutions for businesses around the world. We encourage curiosity, collaboration, and continuous learning.

Hiring Process

  1. Application Screening
  2. Technical Round
  3. HR Round
  4. Offer
Screening
Our recruitment team reviews your resume and application against the requirements of the position.
Technical Round
Candidates demonstrate their technical knowledge through discussions, coding tasks, or role-specific exercises.
HR Round
The HR team discusses your experience, goals, culture fit, and expectations.
Offer
Successful candidates receive an offer and information about joining the company.

Recruiter Tips

  • Keep your resume clear and up to date.
  • Highlight projects related to the position.
  • Be prepared to explain your technical decisions.
  • Research the company before your interview.
  • Ask thoughtful questions during the interview.
