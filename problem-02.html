OVERVIEW

  • LinkedIn is the world's largest professional networking platform, connecting over 1 billion members globally

  • job Searching: Used by individuals to find jobs, display resumes, and connect with recruiters

BENEFITS

  • Recruiter Visibility: A complete profile makes it easy for employers and recruiters to find you for open roles
  • Online Resume: Your profile acts as a live, digital CV that highlights your work history, skills, and projects
  • Easy Applications: You can apply to many job listings quickly using stored profile data

HIRING STAGES

  1. Application Search open jobs to find the right opportunity for you and express your interest by submitting an application. Once your application is received, our recruiting team will review your application to see if you meet all basic and some preferred qualifications listed on the job description.
  2. Conversation A member of our recruiting team manages every job opening. If you meet the qualifications and are selected to move forward, they'll reach out to schedule a call to learn more about you and ask some basic questions. After the initial call, we may also schedule a video call with the hiring manager or another team member. At this stage, they’ll ask more detailed questions about your experience, skills, and technical expertise.
  3. Interview If you are selected to move to the interview phase, you’ll next meet with several more team members. Each interview will focus on a different skill area and is a great opportunity to meet new people, learn more about the role and ask any questions you have. Depending on the role, you may be asked to present a case study or do a whiteboard exercise too - your recruiter will share everything you need to know ahead of time. Every interviewer takes notes to share back with the hiring team

technical round

  1. Online Assessment (OA): Usually features coding questions (easy, medium, and hard levels) focusing on arrays, binary search, and basic algorithms
  2. Screening / Eliminator Round: A preliminary technical or coding round that filters candidates before the full interview loop
  3. Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA): One or two rounds testing your ability to write clean, working code and explain your logic

HR ROUND

  • Tell me about yourself: Share a short summary of your professional journey, key achievements, and goals.
  • Strengths and weaknesses: Pick real traits, show how you use your strengths, and explain how you improve your weaknesses
  • Why do you want to work here?: Connect your personal values and career goals to the company's mission

Screening

  • Screening Questions: Employers can add criteria like years of experience, work authorization, certifications, and language proficiency during job posting
  • Must-Have Qualifications: Setting strict requirements lets LinkedIn automatically flag or reject applications that do not match the baseline criteria within three days
  • Free vs. Paid Limits: Standard personal posts typically include one free screening question, while promoted jobs unlock more advanced filtering options
