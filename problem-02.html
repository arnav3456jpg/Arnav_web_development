Spice Garden Restaurant

Welcome to Spice Garden, where traditional flavors meet modern dining. Enjoy delicious Indian cuisine prepared with fresh ingredients and authentic spices.

Starters

  • Paneer Tikka - Grilled cottage cheese with spices
  • Vegetable Samosa - Crispy pastry filled with vegetables
  • Chicken Seekh Kebab - Spiced minced chicken grilled on skewers

Main Course

  • Butter Chicken - Creamy tomato-based chicken curry
  • Paneer Butter Masala - Cottage cheese cooked in rich gravy
  • Vegetable Biryani - Fragrant rice with vegetables and spices

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun - Soft milk dumplings in sugar syrup
  • Rasmalai - Soft cheese dumplings in sweet milk
  • Kheer - Traditional Indian rice pudding

Beverages

  • Mango Lassi - Refreshing mango yogurt drink
  • Masala Chai - Indian tea with aromatic spices
  • Fresh Lime Soda - Fresh lime with sparkling water

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

  1. Begin with our crispy Vegetable Samosa.
