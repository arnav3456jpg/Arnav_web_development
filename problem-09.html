Student terms and rules.

General rules :

All registered students must carry their valid student ID card at all times while on university premises.This card serves as your primary access key to campus buildings, libraries, and computer labs.Lending your ID card to another individual is strictly prohibited and will result in an immediate suspension of campus privileges.

Attendance :

Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible for final examinations.This threshold ensures you engage fully with the lecture materials and practical sessions.Failure to meet this minimum requirement will result in an automatic grade forfeiture for that specific subject

Examination:

All students must present their official hall ticket and ID card before entering the testing hall.Invigilators check these items to verify your identity and confirm exam registration.Arriving more than 15 minutes after the start time will result in an immediate denial of entry to the exam.

Assignment submission :

All students must submit their digital files through the online portal before the scheduled due date.The portal system tracks and logs timestamps automatically to ensure a fair evaluation process.Late work submitted without prior approval will face a penalty of 10% mark deduction per day past the deadline.

Discipline :

All individuals must show respect to faculty, staff, and peers at all times while on university property.Maintaining a respectful environment ensures a safe, productive, and welcoming space for everyone to learn.Engaging in verbal harassment or physical misconduct will result in immediate expulsion from the university campus.

Library rules :

All library patrons must maintain absolute silence within designated study zones.This quiet environment provides a focused and productive workspace for all students.Removing books without checking them out at the front desk will result in an immediate suspension of library borrowing rights.
