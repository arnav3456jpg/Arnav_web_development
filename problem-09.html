Training Programs

Beginner Program

Designed for athletes who are starting their sporting journey. The program focuses on basic fitness, movement and fundamental techniques.

Intermediate Program

Designed for athletes who already have basic skills and want to improve their strength, speed, technique and performance.

Advanced Performance Program

Intensive training for competitive athletes preparing for tournaments and professional-level competition.

Training Steps

  1. Complete athlete registration.
  2. Attend fitness and skill assessment.
  3. Select a suitable training program.
  4. Attend regular coaching sessions.
  5. Complete fitness and performance tests.
  6. Participate in practice matches.
  7. Prepare for tournament competition.

Competition Terms

Match
A sporting contest between two players or teams.
Fixture
The scheduled arrangement of a particular match.
League
A competition where participants play multiple matches to determine rankings.
Knockout
A competition format where a participant is eliminated after losing a match.
