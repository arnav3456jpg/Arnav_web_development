Welcome to the National Tech & Career Expo

Event Introduction

The National Tech & Career Expo is a major career and technology event designed to connect students, graduates, professionals, companies and innovators. Discover career opportunities, internships, workshops, certifications and networking opportunities.

15 October 2026 to 17 October 2026 at the National Convention Centre.

Why Attend?

  • Meet leading technology companies.
  • Find internships and placement opportunities.
  • Attend practical technology workshops.
  • Meet experienced speakers and industry experts.
  • Build a professional network.
  • Discover certification programs.

About the Expo

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between education and industry by giving learners direct access to employers, mentors and modern technology.

"The future belongs to those who prepare for it today."

The expo focuses on innovation, employability, entrepreneurship, digital skills and lifelong learning.

Preparation Tips

  • Carry several copies of your updated resume.
  • Prepare a short professional introduction.
  • Research companies before meeting recruiters.
  • Wear suitable professional clothing.
  • Bring your student or identity card.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters and speakers.

Tip: Arrive early to avoid queues and attend the opening keynote.
