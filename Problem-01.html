UNIVERSITY INTRODUCTION

  • GLA University is a highly-ranked, government-recognized private state university in India
  • It traces its origins back to 1998 when it was founded by Shri Narayan Das Agrawal under the Sri Jagannath Prasad Ganeshi Lal Bajaj Charitable Trust Samiti
  • It was formally established as a full university in 2010 via the Uttar Pradesh State Legislature

PROGRAM DESCRIPTION

  • offers over 110 undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programs across engineering, management, pharmacy, computer applications, and sciences
  • B.Tech (Computer Science & Engineering): An 8-semester program featuring future-ready specializations like Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Cybersecurity starting from the second year. There are also dual-specialization tracks in collaboration with tech partners like Intel and NEC
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA): A two-year management degree balancing core theory with practical case studies, offering specialized tracks like Business Analytics, Financial Markets & Banking, and Logistics & Supply Chain Management

ELIGIBILITY

  • B.Tech: Pass 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, plus Chemistry or Computer Science, with at least 50% marks in those subjects combined and 50% overall. Requires GLAET or JEE Main
  • BBA / BCA / B.Com: Pass 10+2 in any relevant stream with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks
  • B.A. LL.B (5-Year): Pass 10+2 in any stream with at least 45% marks
