GLA University Profile

GLA University, Mathura

A Premier Institution for Higher Education and Academic Excellence.

About the University

GLA University, located in the holy city of Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, was established by the Sri Jagannath Prasad Ganeshi Lal Bajaj Charitable Trust Samiti to deliver top-tier professional education. As a premier institution accredited with an NAAC 'A+' Grade, the university has built a legacy of academic excellence, discipline, and high employability over nearly three decades. Spanning a sprawling 110-acre eco-friendly campus, it stands as one of North India's most trusted university brands, dedicated to nurturing innovation, research, and future-ready global professionals.

Academic Programs

The university offers a diverse portfolio of over 110 undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programs across multiple disciplines. Students can pursue specialized degrees in Engineering (including advanced B.Tech courses run in collaboration with IBM), Business Management, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Legal Studies, and Computer Applications. Every curriculum focuses on action-based learning and industry integration, utilizing 157 state-of-the-art laboratories to ensure graduates possess strong practical skills matching global corporate standards.

Campus Life

Life at the vibrant Mathura campus offers a balanced environment where student development extends far beyond traditional classrooms. Known affectionately as the home of the "GLAdiators," the campus boasts active student clubs for sports, literature, and cultural activities alongside major annual festivals like Spandan and Tech Navya. With modern amphitheater-style classrooms, multiple auditoriums, secure residential hostels, and an emphasis on holistic growth, students experience an engaging community that prepares them for real-world collaboration.

Admission Process

The path to joining this prestigious institution involves a streamlined application process focused on identifying driven academic talent. All prospective applicants must successfully clear the university's online entrance test (GLAET) or present valid national exam scores to secure their seats. Please note that the final portal registration deadline for the upcoming academic session concludes on July 31st.
