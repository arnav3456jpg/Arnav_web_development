🎉 Annual College Fest 2026 🎉

Event Date: 15th - 17th October 2026

Venue: ABC College Campus

Theme: "Create. Compete. Celebrate."

1. Fest Introduction

Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day celebration that brings together students from different colleges to showcase their talents, compete in exciting events and create unforgettable memories.

The fest includes cultural performances, technical competitions, sports activities, music, dance, quizzes and many other exciting events.

"Our college fest is not just a competition; it is a platform where ideas, talent and friendships come together."
— Fest Organizing Committee

4. Registration Process

Follow the steps below to register for the Annual College Fest 2026.

  1. Visit the official fest registration page.
  2. Create your participant account.
  3. Enter your personal and college details.
  4. Select the competition or event.
  5. Choose individual or team participation.
  6. Pay the applicable registration fee.
  7. Submit the registration form.
  8. Save your registration ID for future reference.

Registration Deadline: 30 September 2026

📧 Register / Contact Registration Team

5. General Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Registration must be completed before the deadline.
  • Participants must report to the venue at least 30 minutes before their event.
  • Participants must follow the instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Any form of misconduct may result in disqualification.
  • The decision of the judging panel will be final.
  • Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.
  • Respect the college campus and maintain cleanliness.

7. Important Definitions

Open Event
An event in which students from any eligible college can participate.
Team Event
A competition in which two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
A competition where a single participant competes.
Registration
The official process of enrolling a participant in a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing an event and assisting participants.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where shortlisted participants compete for the final positions.
