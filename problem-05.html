Event Overview

About the Career & Education Fair

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a major platform for students, graduates and job seekers to discover career opportunities, internships, scholarships and further education programs.

Visitors can meet representatives from companies, universities and training institutions. The event also provides opportunities for career guidance, networking and professional development.

The fair will take place over 15 October 2026 to 17 October 2026.

Event Objectives

  • Connect students with potential employers.
  • Provide information about higher education.
  • Promote internship and apprenticeship opportunities.
  • Help participants understand different career paths.
  • Provide networking opportunities.
  • Offer professional career guidance.

Important Information

Registration is recommended before attending. Participants should bring a valid identification document and several copies of their updated resume.

Event entry is free for registered students. Seats for selected workshops may be limited.

Preparation Guide

Tips for Attendees

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Dress appropriately for professional meetings.
  • Prepare a short personal introduction.
  • Carry identification and academic documents.
  • Prepare questions for employers and universities.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before your first session.
  • Collect contact details for future communication.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register online.
  2. Receive registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue.
  4. Complete check-in.
  5. Visit organization booths.
  6. Attend workshops and presentations.
  7. Network with employers and educators.
  8. Follow up after the event.

Preparation Notice

Tip: Keep your resume concise and highlight your most relevant skills and achievements.

Information & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is registration required?

Yes. Online registration is recommended so that your entry can be processed quickly at the venue.

Who can attend?

Students, graduates, job seekers and anyone interested in career or education opportunities can attend.

Should I bring my resume?

Yes. Bringing several copies of an updated resume is strongly recommended.

Is there an entry fee?

Entry is free for registered participants.

Can I attend multiple sessions?

Yes. Participants can attend multiple sessions as long as there are available seats.
