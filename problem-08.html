Understanding Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account allows you to deposit money securely while earning interest on your balance.

Savings accounts are commonly used for emergency funds, short-term goals and regular savings.

Current Account

Current accounts are generally designed for frequent transactions and business-related banking activities.

Fixed Deposit

A fixed deposit allows you to invest a specific amount for a fixed period at a predetermined interest rate.

Steps to Open a Bank Account

  1. Choose a suitable bank and account type.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Prepare the required identity documents.
  4. Complete the account-opening application.
  5. Submit the required documents.
  6. Complete verification.
  7. Make the initial deposit if required.
  8. Receive your account details.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Track your income and expenses.
  • Build an emergency fund.
  • Pay credit-card bills on time.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Compare interest rates before borrowing.
  • Save regularly for future goals.
  • Protect your banking passwords and PINs.

Understanding Credit Cards

What is a Credit Card?

A credit card allows you to make purchases using a line of credit provided by the card issuer.

You generally need to repay the amount used according to the card's billing terms.

Responsible Credit Card Usage

  • Pay your bill before the due date.
  • Avoid spending beyond your ability to repay.
  • Understand interest and other charges.
  • Review your monthly statement.
  • Keep your card information secure.

"A credit card should be treated as a financial tool, not as extra income."

— Personal Finance Education Principle

Understanding Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan can be used for eligible personal expenses and is generally repaid through scheduled instalments.

Home Loan

A home loan is generally used to purchase, construct or improve residential property, subject to lender terms and eligibility.

Education Loan

Education loans can help eligible students finance approved education-related expenses.

Important Loan Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on the borrowed amount. Example: 8% per year.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment is the regular amount paid toward a loan.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed.
Tenure
The period over which the loan is scheduled to be repaid.
