DEPARTMENT Academic PORTAL:Computer Science Department

INTRODUCTION

Welcome to the Computer Science Department Academic Portal. This portal provides students with important academic information, including the weekly timetable, subjects, faculty details and classroom rules.

Sequence Before Practical Class

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Prepare the required theory concepts.
  3. Bring your practical record.
  4. Open the required software and tools.
  5. Complete the practical task.
  6. Submit the completed work to the faculty.

Classroom Rules

  • Attend classes regularly.
  • Be present before the class begins.
  • Carry your required textbooks and notebooks.
  • Maintain silence while the faculty is teaching.
  • Do not use mobile phones during lectures.
  • Keep the classroom clean.
  • Submit assignments before the deadline.
  • Respect faculty members and fellow students.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session where the faculty explains theoretical concepts to students.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or programming exercises.
Tutorial
A guided academic session used to solve problems and clarify difficult concepts.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, presentations or practical work.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity where students apply theoretical knowledge.

Academic Rules

  1. Students must maintain the required attendance percentage.
  2. Students must submit assignments within the specified deadline.
  3. Students must carry their identity card on campus.
  4. Students must follow examination and assessment guidelines.
  5. Plagiarism and academic dishonesty are strictly prohibited.
