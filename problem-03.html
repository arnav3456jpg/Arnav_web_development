Heavy Rainfall Affects ,Delhi Life :

Schools Remain Closed as Weather Department Issues Alert :

1-Delhi received heavy rainfall on 26 August 2026, causing waterlogging in several parts of the city. Roads became crowded as many vehicles moved slowly through flooded areas.Emergency teams were sent to the affected areas to help people facing difficulties. Workers cleared blocked drains and removed water from important roads. The police also managed traffic near busy markets, hospitals, and metro stations. Many residents appreciated the quick response from the local authorities.

2-The weather department issued a red alert for Delhi because more rain is expected. Schools were closed for one day to keep students safe.Several train and flight services were delayed because of the bad weather. Office workers found it difficult to reach their workplaces on time, while some shops opened late in the morning. The continuous rainfall affected normal life across many parts of Delhi. The government requested people to work from home whenever possible.

3- People have been advised to avoid unnecessary travel and stay indoors if possible. Everyone should follow official weather updates and take care during the heavy rainfall.According to the weather department, rain may continue for the next two days. Citizens are advised to keep umbrellas, raincoats, and emergency supplies readyPeople should avoid standing near electric poles or walking through deep water.Officials will continue to share updates to ensure everyone remains safe.
